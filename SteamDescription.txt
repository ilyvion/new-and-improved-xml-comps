This is a mod that will mostly be a collection of new comps (as well as improvements to vanilla comps) that will let modders do things with XML only that would normally require C# coding. If you're an aspiring mod author in need of a feature that vanilla comps do not offer, drop me a suggestion and I'll let you know whether or not I can add it to the mod for you!

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/CompImprovedSpawner]CompImprovedSpawner[/url] -- a version of the vanilla CompSpawner that allows the spawner to require fuel.
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/CompProperties_ImprovedPower]CompProperties_ImprovedPower[/url] -- a version of the vanilla CompProperties_Power that lets you hide the wires drawn between a building and the conduits.
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/GravshipCooldownExtension]GravshipCooldownExtension[/url] -- a modExtensions entry that lets you replace a GravEngine's cooldown graphic, which is hardcoded by vanilla RW, and change its glow while on cooldown.
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/GravshipOrbExtension]GravshipOrbExtension[/url] -- a modExtensions entry that lets you replace a GravEngine's orb graphic, which is hardcoded by vanilla RW.
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/CompProperties_EmbryoGestation]CompProperties_EmbryoGestation[/url] -- a comp for growth vats that changes how long an embryo takes to gestate.
[\*][url=https://github.com/ilyvion/new-and-improved-xml-comps/wiki/NewAndImprovedXmlComps_PowerGenFactor]NewAndImprovedXmlComps_PowerGenFactor[/url] -- a stat that scales a building's power output, e.g. through a facility's statOffsets.
[/list]
